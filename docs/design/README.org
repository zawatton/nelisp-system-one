by a doc landing -- these gate the work, they are not the work.

** Conventions inherited from the sibling repositories

- A check that has never been seen to fail is not evidence.  Every gate ships
  with a negative control that makes it red.
- Negative results are reported, in the doc, next to the positive ones.
- Under the NeLisp standalone reader, a =PASS maxdiff=0.00e+00= is suspect until
  the helper is known to count NaN as a failure.
