#+title: nelisp-system-one

* Overview

=nelisp-system-one= is an experiment in building a *System One model* in Emacs
Lisp / NeLisp: a model that takes unstructured state as input and returns a
*typed, probabilistic decision* in a single forward pass, instead of generating
text one token at a time.

The category was named by TypeSafe AI when they announced =Jev= on 2026-09-15.
*This repository does not reproduce Jev.*  Jev's weights are closed, its
training method (RLCD) has no published details, and its speed and price figures
describe a hosted cluster.  What is reproducible is the method: a typed output
space, one parallel pass, and calibrated probabilities.  See
[[file:docs/design/01-system-one.org][docs/design/01-system-one.org]] §1 for the claim-by-claim breakdown, and read it
before repeating any vendor number.

* Status

P0 shipped 2026-09-19 -- the evaluation harness and the type gate, with the
stubs and mutations that make both of them evidence.  P0 deliberately builds
the instruments before the thing they measure, because a calibration gate that
reports "well calibrated" for a 4x-overconfident model looks exactly like one
that works.

P1 shipped 2026-09-19 -- a frozen Qwen3-0.6B, pooled, with a linear Noul head,
reaches *0.857* held-out accuracy against a majority baseline of *0.500*, and
ECE 0.051 falling to 0.040 after temperature scaling.  On the compositional
subset, where both members of a pair share their polarity words and only the
composition differs ("failed to lose" against "failed to win"), it reaches
*0.792* over 48 examples where a bag of words scores 0.507.  So the donor is a
usable decision encoder, and what it pools is sentence meaning rather than
word identity.

Four things came out of it: mid depth beats the final layer; the learned
attention pool is not decided by these runs at all, because standardising its
inputs swaps which depth fails; the calibration step was leaking twice over in
ways that returned plausible numbers rather than errors; and the run was three
times slower than the hardware required, in two places that had nothing to do
with the model.  Both leaks were caught by running the probe over synthetic
noise, where every held-out number must come out at chance.

P3 accepted 2026-09-20, on its second statistic.  Its own ECE gate failed and
was then shown unmeasurable at this sample size: a model calibrated by
construction scores 0.043 to 0.115 on 84 examples, against a gate of 0.05.
The binning-free replacement -- how much a fresh recalibrator can still
improve the log-loss -- works there, and says the raw head was miscalibrated
and the temperature-corrected one is not.

=make test= runs 129 checks and needs no model weights.

Scope, settled 2026-09-19: *(A) spike*.  Done means the method demonstrably
works at toy scale, with honest numbers including the negative ones -- phases
P0-P3 of Doc 1.  The deliverable is knowledge.  Integration with anvil (P4) is
out of scope; nothing here may be described as ready to route real traffic.

* The three primitives

| primitive | question                                   | answer                                  |
|-----------+--------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------|
| =Choice=  | pick one of a declared option set (≤255)   | option + a probability per option + confidence |
| =Score=   | rate against ordered, described levels     | level + a probability per level + confidence |
| =Noul=    | a yes/no question                          | P(yes) + confidence                     |

All three are one head: =Noul= is =Choice= over two options, =Score= is =Choice=
with an ordering imposed on them.

* What it cannot do

Generate strings.  A System One model cannot summarise, explain itself, or
answer an open question -- that is the trade that buys the single pass.  It is a
decision layer, not a replacement for a language model.

* Substrate

This repository consumes its siblings rather than forking them:

- =nelisp-llm= -- the imported Qwen3-0.6B donor (int8), LoRA over the frozen
  base, the Qwen tokenizer, the NeLisp compatibility layer.
- =nelisp-photon= -- tensor core, autograd, weight IO.
- =nelisp-gpu= -- SPIR-V/Vulkan kernels, reached through =nelisp-llm=.

* Running the tests

#+end_src

#+begin_src sh
make test PHOTON=/path/to/nelisp-photon/lisp LLM=/path/to/nelisp-llm/lisp
#+end_src
